# Kids Learning Mathematics — Rebuilt Package

This package was rebuilt directly from the two original HTML files supplied in the project:

- `New update math(1).html` → Elementary / beginner lessons
- `Tens and units(2).html` → T U and H T U lessons

## Structure

```text
Kids-Learning-Maths/
├── index.html
├── Elementary/
│   ├── numbers-counting.html
│   ├── addition.html
│   ├── subtraction.html
│   ├── multiplication.html
│   ├── division.html
│   ├── fractions.html
│   └── word-problems.html
├── T-U/
│   ├── addition.html
│   ├── subtraction.html
│   ├── multiplication.html
│   └── division.html
├── H-T-U/
│   ├── addition.html
│   ├── subtraction.html
│   ├── multiplication.html
│   └── division.html
└── Source-Originals/
    ├── New update math(1).html
    └── Tens and units(2).html
```

The topic pages retain the original teaching UI, speech, board/handwriting, checkpoints/practice, progress and lesson content from their respective source. The Elementary pages are restricted to their selected built-in Elementary topic; T U and H T U pages are restricted to their selected operation.

For GitHub Pages, upload the extracted contents so `index.html`, `Elementary/`, `T-U/`, and `H-T-U/` are directly in the published root.
